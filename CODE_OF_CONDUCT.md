# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TraceLens contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Participation should be respectful regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, honest, and professional.
- Respect differing experiences and technical opinions.
- Give and receive specific, constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect boundaries, confidentiality, and privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, sustained disruption, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. TraceLens discussions must never be used to expose private browsing data, credentials, or vulnerability details.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns through a private repository contact channel when one is configured. Until then, request private maintainer contact in a minimal public issue without naming involved people or including sensitive details. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This lightweight policy follows the values and enforcement approach of [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/) without reproducing it verbatim.
